The Story
Retail 24 is a global retail company operating across Americas, Europe, and Asia — from US state offices to markets in Brazil, Germany, and Singapore. This cube contains the full Income Statement: from gross revenue down to net profit, with actuals from 2022–2025 and actuals through Sep 2026 (IST), plus Budget, Forecast & Target through Dec 2026. Everything is reported in USD (base currency) by default, but local currency views are always one click away.
